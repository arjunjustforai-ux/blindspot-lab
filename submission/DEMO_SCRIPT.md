Record the **real running app** (local `scripts/start_dev.sh` or a deployment) in a 1440×900 browser window, plus a terminal for the upload and test shots. Speak in English or add English subtitles. Upload to YouTube as **Public or Unlisted (not Private)**. Narration below is about 130 words per minute; trim if needed.
